## Government Audit & Compliance Management System
**Challenge:** Fragmented intake, review, deficiency, recommendation, CAP, milestone, evidence, and closure records.

**Plan:** Deficiency → Recommendation → CAP → Milestone → Evidence data model; workflow states; validation; audit history; alerts; closure criteria; executive reporting.

**Execution:** Power Apps for intake/review/management; Power Automate for status history, routing, alerts, and escalation; SharePoint/Dataverse for records/evidence; Power BI for health, overdue exposure, completion, and forecasting.

**Demonstration Results:** status-change audit history; automated next-action routing; overdue/ECD visibility; closure blocked until required actions/evidence are complete; portfolio-to-record drill-down.

**Measures:** submission-to-review time • overdue count • workflow aging • CAP completion • evidence completeness • closure cycle • 30/60/90-day exposure
