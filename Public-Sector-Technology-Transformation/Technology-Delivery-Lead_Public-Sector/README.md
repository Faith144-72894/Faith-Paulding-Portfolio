# Public Sector Technology Delivery Lead

## Scenario
Customer delivery spans product, data, security, integrations, vendors, and customer decisions. Risks and issues are reported, but recovery actions are inconsistent.

## Solution
I operate one RAID and recovery control connecting severity, business impact, owner, due date, dependency, decision, recovery action, and closure evidence.

## Execution Logic
New blocker → assess impact → assign accountable owner → identify recovery path → establish due date → escalate by threshold → validate recovery → close with evidence.

## Measures
High-impact blockers • blocker age • overdue decisions • dependency age • recovery closure • delivery variance.

## Continuity
A red status is incomplete unless it includes what keeps the business operating while the root issue is resolved.
